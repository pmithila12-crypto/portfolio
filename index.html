Image Annotation Internship

My practical learning experience

My internship journey in Image Annotation introduced me to the process of identifying, labelling, and organising visual data. This experience helped me understand how accurately labelled images can support computer vision and Artificial Intelligence applications.

Understanding Image Annotation

Learned the basics of image annotation and why accurate image labelling is important for preparing data for AI models.

Identifying Visual Objects

Explored how objects and visual elements can be identified and labelled in images according to the assigned annotation guidelines.

Accuracy and Quality

Understood the importance of attention to detail, consistency, and following instructions while working with image data.

My Learning Outcomes

Gained an introduction to visual data preparation and developed an appreciation for the role of labelled data in AI and computer vision.

Skills I Developed

Image Annotation Visual Data Labelling Attention to Detail Data Accuracy AI Fundamentals Computer Vision Basics
